Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. BetterLifter has no sign-up. Your data stays on your devices and, if you're signed in to iCloud, syncs through your own private iCloud.

My workouts aren't syncing between iPhone and Mac.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on for BetterLifter (Settings → [your name] → iCloud). Sync can take a few minutes, and it waits for a network connection. Opening the app on each device helps it catch up.

How do I use BetterLifter on Apple Watch?

Install the watch app from the Watch app on your iPhone (My Watch → Available Apps). When you start a workout on your phone it appears on your watch, and you can log sets from either one. Your watch shows heart rate while a workout is in progress.

Workouts aren't showing up in Apple Health.

Go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → BetterLifter and turn on Workouts. Sessions are saved to Health when you finish them.

Bodyweight exercises show the wrong load.

BetterLifter reads your latest body weight from Apple Health. Log a current weight in Health, and allow BetterLifter to read Body Weight under Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Dictation isn't working.

Allow microphone and speech recognition access under Settings → Privacy & Security. Transcription runs on your device, so the first use may need to download your language's speech model.

How do I log extra work for one side?

When adding an exercise, pick Left only or Right only. One-side sets are tracked separately, so your history and personal records stay accurate for each side.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the data on that device. To remove synced data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → BetterLifter. Workouts saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
