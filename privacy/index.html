Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

The short version BetterLifter doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your workouts stay on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage, which we can't read.

This policy covers the BetterLifter apps for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac, along with their widgets (together, “the app”). BetterLifter is made by Kaushik Mahadevan (“we”, “us”).

What the app stores

To work as a training log, the app keeps what you enter or record:

  • Your workouts and schedules: exercises, sets, reps, weights, notes and how a session felt.
  • Gyms you save, with their location, and machine settings.
  • Your settings and preferences, such as weight units and reminders.

This data is stored on your device. It never goes to us. Where it can go:

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, the app syncs your data between your devices through Apple's CloudKit, in the private database of your own iCloud account. Only you can access it. We can't see it. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy. To turn sync off, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud on your device.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app:

  • reads your latest body weight, to work out loads for bodyweight exercises;
  • on Apple Watch, reads heart rate and active energy during a workout, to show them live;
  • saves finished sessions to Health as strength training workouts.

Health data stays on your device and in Apple Health. It isn't shared with anyone, and it's never used for advertising, marketing or data mining. You can change or revoke access at any time under Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → BetterLifter.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location while it's open to work out which of your saved gyms you're at, then tags the workout with that gym. The location is used on your device and isn't stored as a history or sent to us. When you search for a gym to add, the search text and the area shown on the map go to Apple Maps to find matches, under Apple's privacy policy.

Microphone and speech

If you dictate a workout or a set, the app uses the microphone and transcribes your speech on your device with Apple's speech recognition. Audio isn't recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Turning dictated text into sets uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model, where available.

Generated artwork

Optional gorilla artwork is created with Apple's Image Playground on your device and saved in the app.

Notifications

Workout reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service of ours is involved.

Imported files

When you import a workout file (CSV or JSON), the app reads it on your device and saves the workouts it finds.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell, rent or share personal data. We don't receive any.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs, and we don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data is yours. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. To delete synced data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → BetterLifter on iPhone, or System Settings → [your name] → iCloud on Mac. Workouts saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Children

BetterLifter isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, at any age.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics of ours. GitHub may log basic request information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, such as the app starting to collect any data, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
